Privacy Policy for Hane

Effective date: September 23, 2026

1. Data collection

Hane does not collect, store on remote servers, sell, or share any personal data. There are no analytics, tracking scripts, or telemetry of any kind in this extension.

2. Local processing only

  • Download history and in-app settings are stored exclusively in your browser's local extension storage (chrome.storage) and never leave your device. You can clear them at any time by removing the extension.
  • Video/audio files, subtitles, and speech-to-text results are saved only to your own device's Downloads folder, at your explicit request.
  • Speech transcription runs 100% locally in your browser using an open-source Whisper model (ONNX Runtime Web, WebAssembly). Audio is never uploaded to any server.

3. Network requests

Hane makes network requests solely to the services you use it with:

  • bilibili.com / douyin.com and their media CDN hosts, to resolve and download the video/subtitle you explicitly requested;
  • huggingface.co / hf-mirror.com, to download the open-source Whisper model weights (one-time; cached locally afterwards).

No other destinations are contacted. Standard request metadata (IP address, User-Agent) is visible to those third-party sites under their own privacy policies; Hane does not attach any user identifiers.

4. Child-directed content

This extension is not directed at children.
